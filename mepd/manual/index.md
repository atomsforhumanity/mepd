# mepd manual

How mepd's features work: what each one computes, the choices made along the
way and why, and what could be done better. The web app links to the
section that explains what you are looking at.

Each page ends with **Limits and possible improvements**. If something here
is wrong, unclear, or could work better for your chemistry, use **Suggest a
change** at the bottom of the page.

## Pages

- [Orbitals](orbitals): the whole system's HOMO and LUMO, and each
  molecule's own orbitals and how they overlap (fragment orbitals).
- [Transition-state conformers](ts-conformers): sampling a TS's other
  arrangements around its reacting core, held fixed, and TS-optimizing each.
